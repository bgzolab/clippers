It’s possible to consider the next event in our lives as something the world
is trying to teach us.

But it might be even more effective to realize that, whenever we choose, we
can _learn_ something from what’s going on. We’re not getting taught, we’re
choosing to learn. There’s a lesson in every interaction, if we want there to
be.

When we choose to learn, our active participation makes a difference.

October 25, 2025
