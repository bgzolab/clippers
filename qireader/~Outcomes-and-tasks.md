It’s difficult to spend the entire day working on outcomes. Sooner or later,
there are tasks to be done, tasks we believe will get us to the outcome we
seek.

But it’s easy to spend the whole day on tasks, failing to recalibrate and
ignoring the fact that the tasks might not be helping us get the results we
set out to create in the first place.
