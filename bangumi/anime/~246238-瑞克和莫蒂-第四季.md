# 瑞克和莫蒂 第四季

The fourth season of the animated television series Rick and Morty was confirmed by Adult Swim in May 2018. The season is set to consist of ten episodes. The first five episodes of the season premiered on November 10, 2019; the remaining five will premiere some time in the future.
