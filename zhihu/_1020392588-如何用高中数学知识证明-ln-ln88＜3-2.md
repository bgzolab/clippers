考虑利用展开式

![\\color{blue}{\\ln\\left\(\\frac{1+x}{1-x}\\right\)=2\\left\(x+\\frac{x^3}{3}+\\frac{x^5}{5}+\\cdots+\\frac{x^{2n+1}}{2n+1}+\\cdots\\right\)\\tag1}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ccolor%7Bblue%7D%7B%5Cln%5Cleft%28%5Cfrac%7B1%2Bx%7D%7B1-x%7D%5Cright%29%3D2%5Cleft%28x%2B%5Cfrac%7Bx%5E3%7D%7B3%7D%2B%5Cfrac%7Bx%5E5%7D%7B5%7D%2B%5Ccdots%2B%5Cfrac%7Bx%5E%7B2n%2B1%7D%7D%7B2n%2B1%7D%2B%5Ccdots%5Cright%29%5Ctag1%7D)

注意到 ![\\ln 88=\\ln 2^6\\cdot\\frac{88}{2^6}=6\\ln
2+\\ln\\left\(\\frac{11}{8}\\right\)\\\\](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cln+88%3D%5Cln+2%5E6%5Ccdot%5Cfrac%7B88%7D%7B2%5E6%7D%3D6%5Cln+2%2B%5Cln%5Cleft%28%5Cfrac%7B11%7D%7B8%7D%5Cright%29%5C%5C)先算
![\\ln 2.](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cln+2.) 在
![\(1\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%281%29) 中命
![x=\\frac{1}{3}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x%3D%5Cfrac%7B1%7D%7B3%7D)
代入，得 ![\\ln2\\approx
2\\left\(\\frac{1}{3}+\\frac{1}{3^4}\\right\)=\\frac{56}{81}\\\\](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cln2%5Capprox+2%5Cleft%28%5Cfrac%7B1%7D%7B3%7D%2B%5Cfrac%7B1%7D%7B3%5E4%7D%5Cright%29%3D%5Cfrac%7B56%7D%7B81%7D%5C%5C)
再算
![\\ln\\left\(\\frac{11}{8}\\right\).](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cln%5Cleft%28%5Cfrac%7B11%7D%7B8%7D%5Cright%29.)
在 ![\(1\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%281%29) 中命
![x=\\frac{3}{19}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x%3D%5Cfrac%7B3%7D%7B19%7D)
代入，得

![\\ln\\left\(\\frac{11}{8}\\right\)
\\approx2\\left\(\\frac{3}{19}+\\frac{3^2}{19^3}\\right\)=\\frac{2184}{6859}\\\\](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cln%5Cleft%28%5Cfrac%7B11%7D%7B8%7D%5Cright%29+%5Capprox2%5Cleft%28%5Cfrac%7B3%7D%7B19%7D%2B%5Cfrac%7B3%5E2%7D%7B19%5E3%7D%5Cright%29%3D%5Cfrac%7B2184%7D%7B6859%7D%5C%5C)
于是

![\\ln 88=6\\ln
2+\\ln\\left\(\\frac{11}{8}\\right\)\\approx6\\cdot\\frac{56}{81}+\\frac{2184}{6859}=4.466561\\cdots\\\\](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cln+88%3D6%5Cln+2%2B%5Cln%5Cleft%28%5Cfrac%7B11%7D%7B8%7D%5Cright%29%5Capprox6%5Ccdot%5Cfrac%7B56%7D%7B81%7D%2B%5Cfrac%7B2184%7D%7B6859%7D%3D4.466561%5Ccdots%5C%5C)
而 ![\\ln
88=4.47734\\cdots\\\\](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cln+88%3D4.47734%5Ccdots%5C%5C)
这表明第一位小数已经达到精确值，但这里我们都只取了 ![\(1\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%281%29)
右端展开式前二项，还可以根据需要添加更多的项进行计算，以达到任意的精度。至于对当前不等式的证明，只需再估计一下误差就可以了，这工作感觉没有多少价值，恕我从略。
