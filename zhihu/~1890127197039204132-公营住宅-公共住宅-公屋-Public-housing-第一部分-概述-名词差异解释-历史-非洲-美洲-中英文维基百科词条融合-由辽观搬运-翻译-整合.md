## 0\. 概述[1]

**公共住宅** （英语：**Public Housing** ），又称**公营住宅** 、**公营房屋** 、**公营屋邨** ，简称**公宅**
、**公屋**
，是指[政府](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E6%2594%25BF%25E5%25BA%259C)直接兴建或委托第三方建造的[住宅](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E4%25BD%258F%25E5%25AE%2585)。世界各地的形式有所不同，通常是[公寓](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E5%2585%25AC%25E5%25AF%2593)型态，由政府出资，以廉价金额[出租](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E5%2587%25BA%25E7%25A7%259F)([社会住宅](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E7%25A4%25BE%25E6%259C%2583%25E4%25BD%258F%25E5%25AE%2585))或[贩售](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E9%258A%25B7%25E5%2594%25AE)予[民众](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E6%25B0%2591%25E7%259C%25BE)，尤其是低收入居民。

**Public housing** , also known as **social housing** , refers to
[subsidized](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsidized_housing)
or [affordable
housing](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affordable_housing)
provided in buildings that are usually owned and managed by [local
government](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government),
[central
government](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_government),
[nonprofit
organizations](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonprofit_organizations)
or a combination thereof. The details, terminology, definitions of
[poverty](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty),
and other criteria for allocation may vary within different contexts, but the
right to
[rent](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renting)
such a home is generally rationed through some form of [means-
testing](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Means-
testing) or through administrative measures of housing
needs.[[2]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-2)
One can regard social housing as a potential remedy for [housing
inequality](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_inequality).
Within the
[OECD](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OECD),
social housing represents an average of 7% of national housing stock (2020),
ranging from ~34% in the Netherlands to less than 1% in
Colombia.[[3]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-3)[[4]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-4)  
【参考译文】公共住房，也称为社会住房，是指通常由地方政府、中央政府、非营利组织或其联合体拥有和管理的补贴性或经济适用住房。在不同背景下，贫困的定义、分配标准及其他细节和术语可能有所不同，但租赁此类住房的权利通常通过某种形式的经济状况调查或住房需求的行政措施来进行限制。[2]
社会住房可以被视为缓解住房不平等的一种潜在解决方案。在经合组织（OECD）国家中，社会住房平均占全国住房存量的7%（2020年数据），从荷兰的约34%到哥伦比亚的不到1%不等。[3][4]

In the United States, public housing developments are classified as **housing
projects** that are owned by a housing authority or a low-income (project-
based voucher) property. PBV are a component of a public housing agency. PBVs,
administered by state and local housing agencies, are distinct from Section 8
Project-Based Rental Assistance (PBRA), a program through which property
owners' contract directly with the Department of Housing and Urban Development
(HUD) to rent units to families with low
incomes.[[5]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-5)  
【参考译文】在美国，公共住房项目被归类为由住房管理局拥有的住房项目或低收入（基于项目的补贴券）物业。基于项目的补贴券（PBV）是公共住房管理局的一个组成部分。PBV由州和地方住房机构管理，与《第八章基于项目的租金援助计划》（PBRA）不同，后者是通过房主直接与住房和城市发展部（HUD）签订合同，向低收入家庭出租单位的计划。[5]

Affordable housing goals can also be achieved through subsidies. [Subsidized
housing](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsidized_housing)

providing affordable housing. Owners may be individual landlords or for-profit
or nonprofit

【参考译文】实现经济适用房目标也可以通过补贴来完成。补贴住房由私人业主拥有和运营，这些业主通过提供经济适用房来获得补贴。业主可以是个人房东，也可以是营利性或非营利性公司。[6]

## 1\. 名词差异解释

公营住宅在各个使用[中文](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E4%25B8%25AD%25E6%2596%2587)的地区有不同称呼，[中国大陆](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E4%25B8%25AD%25E5%259C%258B%25E5%25A4%25A7%25E9%2599%25B8)称为廉租房，[香港](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E9%25A6%2599%25E6%25B8%25AF)称为[公共屋邨](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E9%25A6%2599%25E6%25B8%25AF%25E5%2585%25AC%25E5%2585%25B1%25E6%2588%25BF%25E5%25B1%258B)（简称公屋，“邨”是“村”的本字[[1]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E5%2585%25AC%25E7%2587%259F%25E4%25BD%258F%25E5%25AE%2585%23cite_note-1)，但后世更通行俗字，此二字在香港用法不同），[澳门](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E6%25BE%25B3%25E9%2596%2580)称为[社会房屋](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E6%25BE%25B3%25E9%2596%2580%25E7%25A4%25BE%25E6%259C%2583%25E6%2588%25BF%25E5%25B1%258B)，[台湾](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E8%2587%25BA%25E7%2581%25A3)称为[国民住宅](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E5%259C%258B%25E6%25B0%2591%25E4%25BD%258F%25E5%25AE%2585)（简称国宅）、公营住宅[[2]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E5%2585%25AC%25E7%2587%259F%25E4%25BD%258F%25E5%25AE%2585%23cite_note-2)、[合宜住宅](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E5%2590%2588%25E5%25AE%259C%25E4%25BD%258F%25E5%25AE%2585)、[社会住宅](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E5%258F%25B0%25E7%2581%25A3%25E7%25A4%25BE%25E6%259C%2583%25E4%25BD%258F%25E5%25AE%2585)等，在[新加坡](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E6%2596%25B0%25E5%258A%25A0%25E5%259D%25A1)和[马来西亚](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E9%25A6%25AC%25E4%25BE%2586%25E8%25A5%25BF%25E4%25BA%259E)则称为[组屋](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E7%25BB%2584%25E5%25B1%258B)（flat）。

在[欧洲](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E6%25AD%2590%25E6%25B4%25B2)亦称之为“社会出租住宅”（Social
Rented
Housing），是指政府直接兴建、补助兴建或民间拥有之适合居住房屋，采“只租不卖”模式，以低于市场租金或免费出租给所得较低的家户或特殊的弱势对象的公营住宅。此一名词定义仅为概念指称，各国中央与地方政府实际运作所使用之名称不尽相同，例如[美国](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E7%25BE%258E%25E5%259C%258B)称之为affordable
housing、[日本](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E6%2597%25A5%25E6%259C%25AC)称公营住宅。

Social housing had existed sporadically prior to modern developments. The
oldest still in use is the 16th-century
[Fuggerei](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuggerei)
in
[Augsburg](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augsburg),
[Bavaria](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavaria).  
【参考译文】在现代发展之前，社会住房曾零星存在。至今仍在使用的最古老社会住房是位于巴伐利亚州奥格斯堡的16世纪福格社区（Fuggerei）。

The origins of modern municipal housing lie in the dramatic [urban population
increase](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanization)
caused by the [Industrial
Revolution](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_Revolution)
of the 19th century. In the large cities of the period, many social
commentators, such as [Octavia
Hill](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octavia_Hill)
and [Charles
Booth](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Booth_%28philanthropist%29)
reported on the squalor, sickness and immorality that arose. [Henry
Mayhew](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Mayhew),
visiting [Bethnal
Green](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethnal_Green),
wrote in _[The Morning
Chronicle](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Morning_Chronicle)_
:  
【参考译文】现代市政住房的起源可以追溯到19世纪工业革命引发的剧烈城市人口增长。在当时的大型城市中，许多社会评论家，如奥克塔维亚·希尔（Octavia
Hill）和查尔斯·布斯（Charles Booth），都报告了贫困、疾病和道德败坏等问题。亨利·梅休（Henry
Mayhew）在访问贝瑟纳尔格林（Bethnal Green）时，在《晨报》（The Morning Chronicle）中写道：

> ... roads were unmade, often mere alleys, houses small and without
> foundations, subdivided and often around unpaved courts. An almost total
> lack of drainage and sewerage was made worse by the ponds formed by the
> excavation of brickearth. Pigs and cows in back yards, noxious trades like
> boiling tripe, melting tallow, or preparing cat's meat, and slaughter
> houses, dustheaps, and "lakes of putrefying [night
> soil](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Night_soil)"
> added to the
> filth.[[7]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-7)  
>
> 【参考译文】“道路未铺设，常常只是小巷，房屋狭小且没有地基，被分割成多户，周围通常环绕着未铺砌的庭院。几乎完全缺乏排水系统和污水处理设施，砖土挖掘形成的池塘更使情况恶化。后院的猪和牛、有害的行业如煮肚肠、熬制动物油脂或制作猫粮，以及屠宰场、垃圾堆和‘腐烂粪便堆积而成的湖泊’加剧了污秽。”[7]

Some
[philanthropists](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philanthropy)
began to provide housing in [tenement
blocks](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartment_building),
and some factory owners built entire villages for their workers, such as
[Saltaire](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saltaire)
in 1853 and [Port
Sunlight](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Sunlight)
in 1888. It was in 1885, after the report from a [royal
commission](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_commission)
in England, that the state first took an interest. This led to the [Housing of
the Working Classes Act of
1885](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_of_the_Working_Classes_Act_1885),
which empowered [Local Government
Boards](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Board)
to shut down unhealthy properties and encouraged them to improve the housing
in their areas.  
【参考译文】一些慈善家开始在公寓楼中提供住房，一些工厂主则为工人建造了整个村庄，例如1853年的萨莱尔（Saltaire）和1888年的阳光港（Port
Sunlight）。直到1885年，在英格兰皇家委员会的一份报告之后，国家才首次对此表现出兴趣。这促成了1885年的《工人阶级住房法》，该法案授权地方政府委员会关闭不健康的房产，并鼓励他们改善所在地区的住房条件。

Nearly 6,000 individuals were crammed into the packed streets, where one child
in four died before his or her first birthday. [Arthur

outcry. Construction of the [Boundary
Estate](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_Estate)
was begun in 1890 by the [Metropolitan Board of

in
1900.[[11]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-11)  
【参考译文】伦敦市公司在1865年于法灵顿路（Farringdon Road）建造了公寓楼。[8] 世界上第一个大规模住房项目[9]
在伦敦建成，以取代首都最臭名昭著的贫民窟之一——旧尼科尔（Old Nichol）。[10]
近6,000人挤在狭窄的街道中，每四个孩子中就有一个在一周岁前夭折。阿瑟·莫里森（Arthur Morrison）撰写了具有影响力的《贾戈的孩子》（A

The success of this project spurred many local councils to embark on similar
construction schemes in the early 20th century. The [Arts and Crafts
movement](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_and_Crafts_movement)
and [Ebenezer
Howard](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebenezer_Howard)'s
[garden city
ideas](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garden_city_movement)

[cottage

such as firstly [Totterdown
Fields](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totterdown_Fields)
and later [Wormholt and Old
Oak](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Oak_and_Wormholt).
The [First World
War](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_World_War)
indirectly provided a new impetus, when the poor physical health and condition
of many urban recruits to the [British
Army](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Army)
was noted with alarm. In 1916, 41% of conscripts were unfit to serve. This led
to a campaign known as _[Homes fit for
heroes](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homes_fit_for_heroes)_
and in 1919 the Government first compelled councils to provide housing,
helping them to do so through the provision of
[subsidies](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsidies),
under the [Housing Act
1919](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_Act_1919).[[12]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-
Hollow_2011-12) Public housing projects were tried out in some European
countries and the United States in the 1930s, but only became widespread
globally after [the Second World
War](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Second_World_War).  
【参考译文】该项目的成功促使许多地方议会在20世纪初启动了类似的建设项目。艺术与工艺运动（Arts and Crafts
movement）和埃比尼泽·霍华德（Ebenezer
Howard）的花园城市理念催生了伦敦郡议会的田园式住宅区，例如最早的托特纳姆菲尔兹（Totterdown
Fields），以及后来的沃姆霍尔特（Wormholt）和老橡树（Old
Oak）。第一次世界大战间接提供了新的推动力，当时英国军队中许多城市新兵的身体健康状况令人震惊。1916年，41%的新兵被认为不适合服役。这导致了一场名为“适合英雄居住的家园”（Homes
fit for heroes）的运动，并在1919年政府首次通过《1919年住房法》强制要求地方议会提供住房，同时通过补贴帮助他们实现这一目标。[12]
公共住房项目在20世纪30年代曾在一些欧洲国家和美国尝试过，但直到第二次世界大战后才在全球范围内广泛普及。

## 3\. 非洲 | Africa

### 3.1 南非 | South Africa

South African apartheid featured high poverty rates in Black South Africans
due to racial segregation and discrimination, so the post-apartheid government
has sought to build social housing for poor black households, in urban areas
with economic
opportunity.[[13]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-13)
The Reconstruction and Development and Breaking New Ground programs have
provided over 3.5 million houses from 1995 to 2020, but they did not fully
meet demand, and were built away from inner urban areas, exacerbating racial
divides.[[14]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-14)[[15]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-15)
The housing shortage was estimated to be 3.7 million units in
2021.[[16]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-16)
The Department of Human Settlements, which facilitates national housing
development, has sought to transition from a housing-focused development model
to a holistic view, including
services.[[17]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-17)  
【参考译文】南非的种族隔离制度由于种族隔离和歧视，导致黑人南非人面临高贫困率。因此，后种族隔离时代的政府致力于在经济机会较多的城市地区为贫困的黑人家庭建造社会住房。[13]
《重建与发展计划》（Reconstruction and Development Programme）和《开拓新天地计划》（Breaking New
Ground）从1995年至2020年间提供了超过350万套住房，但这些住房并未完全满足需求，且大多建在远离城市中心的地区，进一步加剧了种族分化。[14][15]
据估计，2021年南非的住房短缺达到370万套。[16] 负责推动全国住房发展的南非人类住区部（Department of Human
Settlements）试图从以住房为中心的发展模式转向更全面的视角，将服务纳入其中。[17]

### 4.1 巴西 | Brazil

_Minha Casa Minha Vida_ ("My House, My Life"), the [Brazilian federal
government's](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Government)
social housing program, was launched in March 2009 with a budget of R$36
billion (US$18 billion) to build one million
homes.[[18]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-18)
The second stage of the program, included within the government [Growth
Acceleration
Program](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Growth_Acceleration_Program)
(PAC, _Programa de Aceleração do Crescimento_) was announced in March

This stage foresaw the construction of a further two million homes.  
【参考译文】“我的家，我的生活”（Minha Casa Minha
Vida），是巴西联邦政府的社会住房计划，于2009年3月启动，预算为360亿巴西雷亚尔（约合180亿美元），目标是建造100万套住房。[18]
该计划的第二阶段被纳入政府的“增长加速计划”（PAC，Programa de Aceleração do
Crescimento），并于2010年3月宣布。[19] 这一阶段预计再建造200万套住房。

All funds for _Minha Casa Minha Vida_ properties were provided by the

Federal](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caixa_Econ%25C3%25B4mica_Federal)_.[[20]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-20)
The bank financed development and provided mortgages for qualifying families.  
【参考译文】所有“我的家，我的生活”项目的资金均由巴西公共银行——联邦经济储蓄银行（Caixa Econômica Federal）提供。[20]
该银行负责资助开发项目，并向符合条件的家庭提供抵押贷款。

As of September 2018, 4.5 million homes were built and distributed to the
population. The project has been criticized for its placement and quality of
the
houses.[[21]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-21)
Houses are built far from the city centre to reduce housing costs which
consequently reduces access to the [labor
market](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labor_market);
a study of randomly selected houses of the MCMV project in [Rio de
Janeiro](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro)

employed.[[22]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-22)  
【参考译文】截至2018年9月，已有450万套住房建成并分配给居民。然而，该项目因其房屋的位置和质量而受到批评。[21]
房屋通常建在远离市中心的地方，以降低住房成本，但这同时也减少了居民进入劳动力市场的机会；一项针对里约热内卢随机选取的“我的家，我的生活”项目房屋的研究表明，求职者正式就业的可能性降低，但对已就业者收入的影响不大。[22]

In addition to addressing the housing deficit, Minha Casa Minha Vida sought to
stimulate the
[economy](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy)
by generating employment in the construction sector. According to the
Brazilian [Chamber of Construction
Industry](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php%3Ftitle%3DChamber_of_Construction_Industry%26action%3Dedit%26redlink%3D1)
(CBIC), the program created millions of jobs, directly and indirectly,
contributing to the country's economic growth during its initial phases.
However, critics argue that the program's focus on mass production sometimes
overlooked the importance of integrating new housing developments with
adequate infrastructure, such as
[schools](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School),
[healthcare
facilities](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_facility),
and [transportation
networks](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_network),
which are essential for fostering sustainable
[urban](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_area)
development.[[23]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-23)  
【参考译文】除了缓解住房短缺问题，“我的家，我的生活”还试图通过在建筑行业创造就业机会来刺激经济。根据巴西建筑业商会（CBIC）的数据，该计划在初期直接和间接创造了数百万个就业机会，为国家经济增长做出了贡献。然而，批评者指出，该计划过于注重大规模生产，有时忽视了将新建住宅与必要的基础设施（如学校、医疗设施和交通网络）相结合的重要性，而这对于推动可持续的城市发展至关重要。[23]

In Canada, public housing is usually a block of purpose-built [subsidized
housing](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsidized_housing)
operated by a government agency, often simply referred to as community
housing, with easier-to-manage townhouses. Many cities in Canada still
maintain large high-rise clustered developments in working-class
neighborhoods, a system that has fallen out of favour in both the United
States and the United Kingdom. However, many public housing corporations still
offer a variety of buildings and communities ranging from individual houses to
townhouse communities and mid-rise and high-rise apartments in both working-
class and middle-class neighborhoods that house a significant number of low-
income Canadians.  
【参考译文】在加拿大，公共住房通常是由政府机构运营的专门建造的补贴住房，常被称为社区住房（community

【参考译文】在公共住房权力下放给地方市政当局之后，安大略省于2002年成立了社会住房服务公司（Social Housing Services

### 4.3 墨西哥 | Mexico

At the end of the Second World War, enriched by US investments and an [oil
boom](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_boom),

Retiro](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direcci%25C3%25B3n_de_Pensiones_Civiles_y_Retiro)_
(the National Pensions office, today ISSSTE), the _Centro Urbano_ (or
_Multifamiliar_) Presidente Alemán (1947–50) in the Colonia del Valle and the
_Centro Urbano_ (or _Multifamiliar_) Benito Juárez (1951–52) in the [Colonia
Roma](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonia_Roma),
introduced formal ideas from [Le
Corbusier](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Corbusier)'s
_[Ville
Radieuse](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ville_Radieuse)_
into the urban fabric.  
【参考译文】第二次世界大战结束后，受益于美国投资和石油繁荣，墨西哥迎来了第一次人口激增，其中农村移民的主要目的地是墨西哥城。当时著名的建筑师马里奥·帕尼·达克伊（Mario

Presidente Alemán, 1947–50）和罗马上区（Colonia Roma）的贝尼托·胡亚雷斯都市中心（Centro Urbano
Benito Juárez, 1951–52）** ，将勒·柯布西耶（Le Corbusier）的“光辉城市”理念引入了城市的结构中。

His later project, the [Conjunto Urbano Tlatelolco
Nonoalco](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjunto_Urbano_Nonoalco_Tlatelolco)
built in 1960–65, was meant to develop one of the poorest parts of the city,
[Santiago

which was becoming a slum. Unfortunately, after a while, instead of giving the
residences to the previous residents of Tlatelolco, corruption took place and

【参考译文】他的后来项目——**诺诺阿尔科-特拉特洛尔科综合都市中心（Conjunto Urbano Tlatelolco Nonoalco,
1960–65）**
，旨在开发城市最贫困的地区之一：圣地亚哥·特拉特洛尔科，该地区当时正逐渐沦为贫民窟。然而，不幸的是，一段时间后，这些住宅并没有分配给原本居住在特拉特洛尔科的居民，而是因腐败问题，大部分住房被分配给了政府雇员。

During the [earthquake of
1985](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_Mexico_City_earthquake),
both the Benito Juárez and Nonoalco-Tlaltelolco complexes suffered major
damage, with some buildings collapsing. Today most of the Multifamiliar Benito
Juárez has been demolished.  
【参考译文】在1985年的地震中，贝尼托·胡亚雷斯和诺诺阿尔科-
特拉特洛尔科两个综合体均遭受了严重损坏，部分建筑甚至倒塌。如今，贝尼托·胡亚雷斯多户住宅的大部分已被拆除。

Mexico has had experience with housing projects since [Porfirio
Díaz](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porfirio_D%25C3%25ADaz)'s
regime (1877–1880, 1884–1911). One of those still remains and is the _Barrio_
of Loreto in [San
Ángel](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_%25C3%2581ngel),
[Álvaro

【参考译文】墨西哥自波菲里奥·迪亚斯（Porfirio
Díaz）政权时期（1877–1880年、1884–1911年）便已有过住房项目的经验。其中一个项目至今仍然存在，那就是墨西哥城阿尔瓦罗·奥夫雷贡区（Álvaro
Obregón）圣安赫尔（San Ángel）的洛雷托街区（Barrio de Loreto），该项目最初是为了一个造纸厂工人而建。

Another notable public housing project is the Conjunto Habitacional
Independencia, located near Tizapán neighborhood, on most of the land that
once was the Matsumoto Hacienda. The project was developed during [Adolfo
López
Mateos](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolfo_L%25C3%25B3pez_Mateos)
presidential period, started in 1959 and completed in 1960. The development

movie theater, a supermarket, a kindergarten, three elementary schools, among
others. It functioned as public housing until 1982, when the houses and
apartments were sold to the residents.  
【参考译文】另一个值得注意的公共住房项目是**独立住宅综合体（Conjunto Habitacional Independencia）**
，位于蒂萨潘（Tizapán）附近，占地大部分曾属于松本庄园（Hacienda Matsumoto）。该项目在阿道夫·洛佩斯·马特奥斯（Adolfo
López
Mateos）总统任期内开发，于1959年开始建设，并于1960年完工。该社区的设计考虑了整体规划，包括绿化景观和为居民提供基本服务的设施：诊所、体育中心、剧院、电影院、超市、幼儿园、三所小学等。直到1982年，该项目一直作为公共住房使用，随后房屋和公寓被出售给居民。

### 4.4 波多黎各 | Puerto Rico

 _Main article:[Public housing in Puerto
Rico](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing_in_Puerto_Rico)【主条目：波多黎各的公共住房】_

![](https://pic3.zhimg.com/v2-20cb80e098f516570db022c8579ebc40_1440w.jpg)此图片属于公共领域

> 图片题注：Public housing project in Ponce, Puerto Rico in 1941  
> 参考译文：1941年波多黎各庞塞的公共住房项目  
> 图片来源：Jack Delano

Neighbourhoods in Puerto Rico are often divided into three types:
_[barrio](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrio)_
, _urbanización_ (urbanisation) and _residencial público_ (public
housing).[[26]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-
primerahora.com-26) An _urbanización_ is a type of housing where land is
developed into lots, often by a private developer, and where single-family
homes are built.  
【参考译文】波多黎各的社区通常分为三种类型：**巴里奥（barrio）** 、**城市化社区（urbanización,
英文为urbanisation）和公共住房（residencial público）** 。[26] **城市化社区**
是一种住房形式，土地被开发成地块，通常由私人开发商完成，并建造独栋住宅。

More recently, non-single-family units, such as
[condominiums](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condominium_%28living_space%29)
and
[townhouses](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townhouse)
are being built which also fall into this
category.[[26]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-
primerahora.com-26) (In Puerto Rico, a condominium is a housing unit located
in a high-rise building. It is popularly called an _apartamento_ (English:
'apartment'), whether or not its resident owns the unit or lives in it as a
renter.)  
【参考译文】近年来，非独栋住宅单元（如公寓和联排别墅）也开始被建造，并归入这一类别。[26]
（在波多黎各，公寓是指位于高层建筑中的住房单元。无论居民是拥有该单元还是租住，它通常被称为“apartamento”，即英文的“apartment”。）

Public housing, on the other hand, are housing units built with government
funding, primarily through programs of the [US Department of Housing and Urban
Development](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Department_of_Housing_and_Urban_Development)
(HUD) and the [US Department of
Agriculture](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Department_of_Agriculture)
(USDA).[[27]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-27)
These have traditionally been multi-family dwellings in housing complexes
called a _Barriada_ or a _Caserío_ (and more recently a _Residencial
Publico_), where all exterior grounds are shared areas.  
【参考译文】另一方面，**公共住房** 是由政府资助建造的住房单元，主要通过美国住房和城市发展部（HUD）和美国农业部（USDA）的项目资助。[27]
传统上，这些住房是以多户住宅的形式存在于名为**巴里亚达（Barriada）或卡塞里奥（Caserío）**
（以及较新的**公共住宅区（Residencial Público）** ）的住宅综合体中，所有外部区域均为共享空间。

Increasingly, however, public housing developments are being built that
consist of other than the traditional multi-family dwellings with all exterior
grounds consisting of shared outside area, for example, public housing may
consist of single family [garden
apartments](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garden_apartment)
units. Finally, a home that is located in neither an _urbanizacion_ nor of a
public housing development is said to be located in (and to be a part of) a
_barrio_.[[26]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-
primerahora.com-26)  
【参考译文】然而，近年来，公共住房的开发逐渐多样化，不再局限于传统的多户住宅，所有外部区域也不一定都是共享空间。例如，公共住房可能包括独栋花园公寓单元。最后，一个既不属于城市化社区也不属于公共住房开发项目的住宅被认为位于（并属于）**巴里奥**
。[26]

In Puerto Rico, a _barrio_ also has a second and very different meaning
official meaning: the geographical area into which a
_[municipios](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipio)_
is divided for official administrative purposes. In this sense,
_urbanizaciones_ as well as public housing developments (as well as one or
several _barrios_ in the popular sense) may be located in one of these 901
official geographic
areas.[[28]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-28)  
【参考译文】在波多黎各，**巴里奥**
还有第二个且完全不同的官方定义：指市政府（municipios）为官方行政目的划分的地理区域。在这个意义上，城市化社区和公共住房开发项目（以及一个或多个通俗意义上的巴里奥）可能位于这901个官方地理区域之一。[28]

### 4.5 美国 | United States

 _Main article:[Subsidized housing in the United
States](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsidized_housing_in_the_United_States)【主条目：美国的补贴住房】_

_主条目：[美国的公营住宅](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%25E7%25BE%258E%25E5%259B%25BD%25E7%259A%2584%25E5%2585%25AC%25E8%2590%25A5%25E4%25BD%258F%25E5%25AE%2585)_

![](https://pic3.zhimg.com/v2-353512ed784ca0993972990715175c62_1440w.jpg)此图片属于公共领域

> 图片题注：The 20-story [John F.
> Hylan](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Francis_Hylan)
> Houses in the
> [Bushwick](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bushwick%2C_Brooklyn)

In the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, government involvement in
housing for the poor was chiefly in the introduction of [building
standards](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Building_standards).
[Atlanta,
Georgia](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta%2C_Georgia)'s
[Techwood
Homes](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Techwood_Homes),
dedicated in 1935, was the nation's first public housing
project.[[29]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-29)
Most housing communities were developed from the 1930s onward and initial
public housing was largely
[slum](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slum)
clearance, with the requirement insisted upon by private builders that for
every unit of public housing constructed, a unit of private housing would be
demolished.  
【参考译文】在19世纪和20世纪初期，政府对贫困人群住房的参与主要体现在建筑标准的引入上。佐治亚州亚特兰大的**Techwood Homes**
于1935年落成，是美国第一个公共住房项目。[29]
大多数住房社区从20世纪30年代开始发展，早期的公共住房主要是贫民窟清理的结果，并且私人开发商要求每建造一个公共住房单元，就必须拆除一个私人住房单元。

This also eased concerns of the establishment by eliminating or altering
neighborhoods commonly considered a source of disease, and reflected
[progressive-
era](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_Era)
sanitation initiatives. Moreover, public housing, along with the [Federal
Highway
Program](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal-
aid_highway_program), demolished the older, substandard housing of communities
of color across the United States.  
【参考译文】这也在一定程度上消除了被认为容易传播疾病的社区，反映了进步时代的卫生倡议。此外，公共住房和联邦公路计划一起拆除了美国各地有色人种社区中老旧且不达标的住房。

However, the advent of makeshift tent communities during the [Great
Depression](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Depression_in_the_United_States)
caused concern in the Administration. The Federal public housing program was
created by the 1937 Act, in which operations were "sustained primarily by
tenant
rents."[[30]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-
auto-30) As a result, public housing in its earliest decades was usually much
more working-class and middle-class and white than it was by the 1970s. Many
Americans associate large, multi-story towers with public housing, but early
projects were actually low-rise, though [Le
Corbusier](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Corbusier)
[superblocks](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_block)
caught on before World War II.  
【参考译文】然而，大萧条时期临时帐篷社区的出现引起了政府的关注。1937年的《住房法案》创建了联邦公共住房项目，其运营“主要依靠租户租金维持”。[30]
因此，在最初的几十年里，公共住房的居民大多是工人阶级、中产阶级和白人，而不是像20世纪70年代那样以低收入群体为主。许多美国人将大型多层塔楼与公共住房联系在一起，但早期的项目实际上是低层建筑，尽管勒·柯布西耶的超街区设计在二战前一度流行。

A unique US public housing initiative was the development of subsidized
middle-class housing during the late [New
Deal](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Deal)
(1940–42) under the auspices of the [Mutual Ownership Defense Housing
Division](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutual_Ownership_Defense_Housing_Division)
of the [Federal Works
Agency](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Works_Agency)
under the direction of [Colonel Lawrence
Westbrook](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonel_Lawrence_Westbrook).
These eight projects were purchased by the residents after the Second World
War and as of 2009 seven of the projects continue to operate as [mutual
housing
corporations](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_cooperative)
owned by their residents. These projects are among the very few definitive
success stories in the history of the US public housing effort.  
【参考译文】美国一个独特的公共住房举措是在新政晚期（1940-42年）开发的中产阶级补贴住房，由联邦工程局（Federal Works
Agency）下属的互助所有权防御住房部门（Mutual Ownership Defense Housing
Division）负责，主管为劳伦斯·韦斯特布鲁克上校（Colonel Lawrence
Westbrook）。这些八个项目的住房在二战后被居民购买，截至2009年，其中七个项目继续作为由居民拥有的互助住房公司运营。这些项目是美国公共住房努力历史上为数不多的成功案例之一。

![](https://pic2.zhimg.com/v2-8aecf96bcdd902bc78484743adce8d35_1440w.jpg)此图片遵循CC
BY 3.0协议

> 图片题注：The [Ramona
> Gardens](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramona_Gardens)
> projects in [Los
> Angeles](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles),
> [California](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California).  
> 参考译文：加利福尼亚州洛杉矶的拉莫纳花园项目  
>
> 图片来源：[Tedder](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User%3ATedder)

Public housing was only built with the blessing of the local government, and
projects were almost never built on suburban
[greenfields](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenfield_land),
but through regeneration of older neighborhoods. The destruction of
[tenements](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenement)
and eviction of their low-income residents consistently created problems in
nearby neighborhoods with "soft" real estate markets.  
【参考译文】公共住房的建设需要得到地方政府的批准，项目几乎从未建在郊区的空地上，而是通过对旧社区的改造实现的。贫民窟的拆除和低收入居民的搬迁常常在附近房地产市场疲软的社区引发问题。

Initiatives in housing policy were implemented in ways that perpetuated stigma
against African Americans. Initially, public housing was intended to be built
widespread, and as such be mixed-income, but lobbyists who did not want to see
public housing decrease their housing values blocked such housing from going
up**[[31]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-31)**.
These early
[NIMBY](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NIMBY)
movements limited where public housing was concentrated: predominantly in low-
income neighborhoods. With the introduction of suburbs and expansion of
choices for the white working class, the demographics of public housing
changed from more class and racially integrated to predominantly impoverished,
single-parent, welfare, and people of
color**[[32]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-%3A02-32)**.
This led to stigmatization of public housing, through pushing the narrative
that people living in public housing were "[Welfare
Queens](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welfare_queen)",
or otherwise living in a state of abject poverty and terrible
conditions**[[32]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-%3A02-32)**.
These demographic changes also decreased support for housing, leading to the
government cutting funding for the
program**[[33]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-33)**.
Because of funding cuts and mismanagement by public housing authorities,
public housing started to reflect modern associated characteristics of "soul-
crushing" buildings or "humanitarian disasters"—to which the 1993 HOPE VI
project's response was
demolition**[[32]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-%3A02-32)**.  
【参考译文】住房政策的实施方式延续了对非裔美国人的污名化。最初，公共住房计划在全国范围内推广，并希望实现混合收入社区，但游说团体担心公共住房会降低他们的房产价值，因此阻止了这种住房的发展。[31]
这些早期的“邻避”运动（NIMBY）限制了公共住房的分布：主要集中于低收入社区。随着郊区的兴起和白人工薪阶层住房选择的扩大，公共住房的人口结构从阶级和种族融合转变为以贫困、单亲家庭、福利领取者和有色人种为主。[32]
这导致了对公共住房的污名化，通过宣传住在公共住房中的居民是“福利女王”，或生活在极度贫困和恶劣条件中来强化这一形象。[32]
这些人口变化也削弱了对公共住房的支持，最终导致政府削减了该项目的资金。[33]
由于资金削减和公共住房当局的管理不善，公共住房逐渐呈现出“令人绝望”的建筑或“人道主义灾难”的现代特征——这也是1993年HOPE
VI项目的回应措施：拆除。

The federal [Housing and Urban
Development](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_and_Urban_Development)
(HUD) department's 1993 [HOPE
VI](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HOPE_VI)
program addressed concerns of distressed properties and blighted superblocks
with revitalization and funding projects for the renewal of public housing to
decrease its density and allow for tenants with mixed income
levels.[[34]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-%25244billiongrants-34)[[35]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-
HUDpressrelease-35) The project paired together the demolition of public
housing stock and private development, leading to the displacement of many
residents.[[36]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-36)
One of the biggest components of this was the repealing of the "one-for-one"
replacement rule, which said that for every unit of housing destroyed a new
one must be
built**[[37]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-%3A0-37)**.
HOPE VI's reasoning for repealing this policy was that it was hindering the
construction of new, mixed-income units: since the public housing buildings
were so massive, those behind HOPE VI believed that trying to match the 'one-
for-one' rule would make building new housing extremely
difficult**[[37]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-%3A0-37)**.
The long-term effect of this was that more housing was demolished than built,
and many people were displaced without being guaranteed a spot in the new
housing that would get built. This led to the widespread displacement and
reshuffling of public house residents: namely, low-income, Black, single-
parent
families**[[32]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-%3A02-32)**.
Narratives that public housing projects were full of crime, drugs, and poverty
were used to further justify demolition and destruction of public housing.
Such associations between crime, surveillance and policing, and the projects
increased in 1996.  
【参考译文】联邦住房和城市发展部（HUD）于1993年推出的HOPE
VI项目旨在通过振兴和资助公共住房的更新来解决破败物业和超街区的问题，降低人口密度并允许混合收入租户入住。[34][35]
该项目结合了公共住房的拆除和私人开发，导致许多居民被迫搬迁。[36]
其中最重要的措施之一是废除了“一换一”的替换规则，即每拆除一个住房单元必须新建一个单元。[37] HOPE
VI的理由是，这一规则阻碍了新建混合收入单元：因为公共住房建筑规模巨大，支持HOPE VI的人认为遵循“一换一”规则会使新住房建设变得极其困难。[37]
这一政策的长期影响是拆除的住房多于新建的住房，许多人被驱逐而没有保证获得新住房的机会。这导致了公共住房居民的大规模迁移和重新安置，尤其是低收入、黑人、单亲家庭。[32]
关于公共住房充斥犯罪、毒品和贫困的叙事进一步被用来证明拆除和破坏公共住房的合理性。这种犯罪、监控和治安之间的关联在1996年变得更加紧密。

Projects continue to have a reputation for violence, drug use, and
prostitution, especially in [New
Orleans](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans),
[Washington,
D.C.](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington%2C_D.C.)
[Chicago](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago)
and
[Detroit](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit),
leading to the passage of a 1996 federal "[one strike you're
out](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_strike_you%2527re_out)"
law, enabling the eviction of tenants convicted of crimes, especially drug-
related, or merely as a result of being tried for some
crimes.[[38]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-38)
Specifically, the Clinton era established, through HUD, the Public Housing
Drug Elimination Program, which led to the cracking down of public housing,
leading to more policing and surveillance for low-income people of
color**[[39]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-39)**.  
【参考译文】公共住房项目仍然因暴力、吸du2和卖yin2而臭名昭著，尤其是在新奥尔良、华盛顿特区、芝加哥和底特律，这促使1996年联邦通过了“一次犯错你就出局”的法律，允许驱逐被判有罪的租户，特别是与毒品相关的犯罪，甚至仅仅是因为被指控某些犯罪。[38]
特别是在克林顿政府时期，通过HUD设立了公共住房毒品清除计划，这导致了对公共住房的严厉打击，增加了对低收入有色人种的警务和监控。[39]

**4.5.1 转向补贴住房 | Turn to subsidized housing**

In the 60s and 70s, the popularization of neoliberalism caused a turn away
from public sector solutions towards private or public-private solutions.
This, in conjunction with the narrative of public housing being obsolete, led
to both the turn away from public housing and towards subsidized housing
solutions.  
【参考译文】在20世纪60年代和70年代，新自由主义的普及使人们从公共部门解决方案转向私人或公私合作解决方案。这一点，再加上公共住房已经过时的叙事，导致了从公共住房转向补贴住房解决方案。

Houses, apartments or other residential units are usually subsidized on a
rent-geared-to-income (RGI) basis. Some communities have now embraced a mixed
income, with both assisted and market rents, when allocating homes as they
become available.  
【参考译文】房屋、公寓或其他住宅单位通常根据收入挂钩租金（RGI, Rent Geared to
Income）进行补贴。一些社区在分配住房时尝试混合收入模式，同时提供补贴租金和市场租金。

A significant change in the program took place in 1969, with the passage of
the Brooke Amendment. Rents now became set at 25% of a tenant's income with
the result that the program began serving the "poorest tenants."  
【参考译文】该计划的一个重要变化发生在1969年，《布鲁克修正案》（Brooke
Amendment）将租金设定为租户收入的25%，结果使得项目开始服务于“最贫穷的租户”。

Other attempts to solve these problems include the
1974[[40]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-40)
[Section
8](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Section_8_%28housing%29)
Housing Program, which encourages the private sector to construct affordable
homes, and subsidized public housing. This assistance can be "project-based",
subsidizing properties, or "tenant-based", which provides tenants with a
voucher, accepted by some landlords. This policy option represented a turn
away from the public-sector policy of public housing, instead turning towards
the private market to address housing needs. The program, in conjunction with
HOPE VI, was intended to create income-integrated communities, by giving
residents the choice of where to
move**[[37]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-%3A0-37)**.
However, the housing voucher program has historically had long wait times and
limited choice on where one can actually
move**[[41]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-%3A03-41)**.
Additionally, it was found that many people of color did not want to move away
from their families, communities, and systems of support, as well as
experiencing stigma and difficulties with landlords, safety, or
expenses**[[41]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-%3A03-41)**.
This leads to the program doing little to actually create a more racially-
integrated city demographics, mostly reproducing inequality while
simultaneously not having enough valid housing units for the long list of
applicants**[[42]](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_housing%23cite_note-42)**.  
【参考译文】其他试图解决这些问题的努力包括1974年的《第8条住房计划》（Section 8 Housing
Program），该计划鼓励私营部门建造经济适用房和补贴公共住房。这种援助可以是“基于项目的”，即补贴物业，也可以是“基于租户的”，即为租户提供房东接受的代金券。这一政策选项代表了从公共住房的公共部门政策转向依赖私人市场解决住房需求。该计划与HOPE
VI相结合，旨在通过让居民选择搬到哪里来创建混合收入社区。[37]
然而，住房券计划历来存在漫长的等待时间和有限的选择权，居民实际可搬入的地方受到限制。[41]
此外，研究发现，许多有色人种不愿意离开家人、社区和支持系统，同时也面临房东的歧视、安全问题或高昂费用等问题。[41]
这导致该计划未能真正创造更加种族融合的城市人口分布，反而在很大程度上复制了不平等，同时无法为长长的申请名单提供足够的有效住房单位。[42]
