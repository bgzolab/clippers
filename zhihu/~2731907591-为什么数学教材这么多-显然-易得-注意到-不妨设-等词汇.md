Princeton曾经流传的数学家们对“显然(obvious)”的定义。  
if Wedderburn says it's obvious, everybody in the room has seen it ten minutes
ago.  

if Bohnenblust says it's obvious, it's obvious.

  
if Bochner says it's obvious, you can figure it out in half an hour.  

if von Neumann says it's obvious, you can prove it in three months if you are
a genius.  

if Lefschetz says it's obvious, it's wrong.
