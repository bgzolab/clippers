## 8.三角（Triangular）不等式

一般形式：

> 对![n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=n) 维向量
> ![\\pmb{x}=\(x_1,\\cdots,x_n\),\\pmb{y}=\(y_1,\\cdots,y_n\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cpmb%7Bx%7D%3D%28x_1%2C%5Ccdots%2Cx_n%29%2C%5Cpmb%7By%7D%3D%28y_1%2C%5Ccdots%2Cy_n%29)
> ，利用两边之和大于第三边： ![ \\big||\\pmb{x}|-|\\pmb{y}|\\big|\\leqslant
> |\\pmb{x}\\pm\\pmb{y}|\\leqslant |\\pmb{x}|+|\\pmb{y}|
> ](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=+%5Cbig%7C%7C%5Cpmb%7Bx%7D%7C-%7C%5Cpmb%7By%7D%7C%5Cbig%7C%5Cleqslant+%7C%5Cpmb%7Bx%7D%5Cpm%5Cpmb%7By%7D%7C%5Cleqslant+%7C%5Cpmb%7Bx%7D%7C%2B%7C%5Cpmb%7By%7D%7C+)  
>  当且仅当
> ![\\pmb{x}\\parallel\\pmb{y}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cpmb%7Bx%7D%5Cparallel%5Cpmb%7By%7D)
> 时，等号成立.  
>  即： ![ \\sqrt{\(x_1\\pm y_1\)^2+\\cdots+\(x_n\\pm
> y_n\)^2}\\leqslant\\sqrt{x_1^2+\\cdots+x_n^2}+\\sqrt{y_1^2+\\cdots+y_n^2}\\\\\\sqrt{\(x_1\\pm
> y_1\)^2+\\cdots+\(x_n\\pm
> y_n\)^2}\\geqslant\\left|\\sqrt{x_1^2+\\cdots+x_n^2}-\\sqrt{y_1^2+\\cdots+y_n^2}\\right|\\\\
> ](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=+%5Csqrt%7B%28x_1%5Cpm+y_1%29%5E2%2B%5Ccdots%2B%28x_n%5Cpm+y_n%29%5E2%7D%5Cleqslant%5Csqrt%7Bx_1%5E2%2B%5Ccdots%2Bx_n%5E2%7D%2B%5Csqrt%7By_1%5E2%2B%5Ccdots%2By_n%5E2%7D%5C%5C%5Csqrt%7B%28x_1%5Cpm+y_1%29%5E2%2B%5Ccdots%2B%28x_n%5Cpm+y_n%29%5E2%7D%5Cgeqslant%5Cleft%7C%5Csqrt%7Bx_1%5E2%2B%5Ccdots%2Bx_n%5E2%7D-%5Csqrt%7By_1%5E2%2B%5Ccdots%2By_n%5E2%7D%5Cright%7C%5C%5C+)

乱序和![\\geqslant](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cgeqslant)
逆序和。所以两者基本表达差不多意思。  
对于![x_1\\geqslant x_2\\geqslant\\cdots\\geqslant
x_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x_1%5Cgeqslant+x_2%5Cgeqslant%5Ccdots%5Cgeqslant+x_n)
和 ![y_1\\geqslant y_2\\geqslant\\cdots\\geqslant
y_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=y_1%5Cgeqslant+y_2%5Cgeqslant%5Ccdots%5Cgeqslant+y_n)
有： ![ \\begin{aligned}x_1y_1+x_2y_2+\\cdots+x_ny_n&\\geqslant
\\frac{1}{n}\(x_1+x_2+\\cdots+x_n\)\(y_1+y_2+\\cdots+y_n\)\\\\&\\geqslant
