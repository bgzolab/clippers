由于 ![f\(x\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%28x%29) 是周期为
![2\\pi](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=2%5Cpi) 的周期函数，我们只需研究其在一个周期内比如
![\[-\\pi,\\pi\]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5B-%5Cpi%2C%5Cpi%5D)
上的值域；同时，又注意到 ![f\(x\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%28x%29)
也是奇函数，于是又只需考虑
![\[0,\\pi\]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5B0%2C%5Cpi%5D) 上的值域，至于
![\[-\\pi,0\]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5B-%5Cpi%2C0%5D)  
上的值域，只需要将它关于原点「翻折」过去就够了。

  * 当 ![x=0,\\pi](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x%3D0%2C%5Cpi) 时， ![f\(x\)=0;](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%28x%29%3D0%3B)
  * 当 ![0<x<\\pi](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=0%3Cx%3C%5Cpi) 时， 注意到 ![-1<\\cos x<1,](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=-1%3C%5Ccos+x%3C1%2C) 可利用均值不等式，得到![\\begin{align*} 4\\sin^2 x\(1+\\cos x\)^2&=4\(1-\\cos x\)\(1+\\cos x\)^3\\\\ &=\\frac{4}{3}\(3-3\\cos x\)\(1+\\cos x\)\(1+\\cos x\)\(1+\\cos x\)\\\\ &\\leq \\frac{4}{3}\\left\[{\\frac{\(3-3\\cos x\)+\(1+\\cos x\)+\(1+\\cos x\)+\(1+\\cos x\)}{4}}\\right\]^4\\\\ &=\\frac{27}{4}, \\end{align*}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%2A%7D+4%5Csin%5E2+x%281%2B%5Ccos+x%29%5E2%26%3D4%281-%5Ccos+x%29%281%2B%5Ccos+x%29%5E3%5C%5C+%26%3D%5Cfrac%7B4%7D%7B3%7D%283-3%5Ccos+x%29%281%2B%5Ccos+x%29%281%2B%5Ccos+x%29%281%2B%5Ccos+x%29%5C%5C+%26%5Cleq+%5Cfrac%7B4%7D%7B3%7D%5Cleft%5B%7B%5Cfrac%7B%283-3%5Ccos+x%29%2B%281%2B%5Ccos+x%29%2B%281%2B%5Ccos+x%29%2B%281%2B%5Ccos+x%29%7D%7B4%7D%7D%5Cright%5D%5E4%5C%5C+%26%3D%5Cfrac%7B27%7D%7B4%7D%2C+%5Cend%7Balign%2A%7D) 进而可得 ![f\(x\)=\\sin 2x+2\\sin x=2\\sin x\(\\cos x+1\)\\leq \\frac{3\\sqrt{3}}{2},](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%28x%29%3D%5Csin+2x%2B2%5Csin+x%3D2%5Csin+x%28%5Ccos+x%2B1%29%5Cleq+%5Cfrac%7B3%5Csqrt%7B3%7D%7D%7B2%7D%2C) 当且仅当 ![x=\\pi/3](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x%3D%5Cpi%2F3) 时成立等式。

于是可得![-\\frac{3\\sqrt{3}}{3}\\leq f\(x\)\\leq \\frac{3\\sqrt{3}}{2},
x\\in\[-\\pi,\\pi\]\\\\](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=-%5Cfrac%7B3%5Csqrt%7B3%7D%7D%7B3%7D%5Cleq+f%28x%29%5Cleq+%5Cfrac%7B3%5Csqrt%7B3%7D%7D%7B2%7D%2C+x%5Cin%5B-%5Cpi%2C%5Cpi%5D%5C%5C)
依前述周期性，这事实上已经给出了 ![x\\in
\\mathbb{R}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x%5Cin+%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D)
的值域，所以 ![\\min_{x \\in \\mathbb{R}}
f\(x\)=-\\frac{3\\sqrt{3}}{2}.](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmin_%7Bx+%5Cin+%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D%7D+f%28x%29%3D-%5Cfrac%7B3%5Csqrt%7B3%7D%7D%7B2%7D.)
