In order to make the proof more formal , we should first be familiar with the
concept of complex series

![\\text{Complex Power
Series}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ctext%7BComplex+Power+Series%7D)

The theory of Taylar series in real-variable calculus associates to each
infinitely differentiable function f from
![\\mathbb{R}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D) to
![\\mathbb{R}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D) a formal
power series expansion at each point of
![\\mathbb{R}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D) ,namely

![\\sum_{n=0}^{\\infty}\\frac{f^{\(n\)}}{n!}\(x-p\)^n,p\\in\\mathbb{R}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Csum_%7Bn%3D0%7D%5E%7B%5Cinfty%7D%5Cfrac%7Bf%5E%7B%28n%29%7D%7D%7Bn%21%7D%28x-p%29%5En%2Cp%5Cin%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D)

There is no general guarantee that this series converges for any
![x](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x) other than
![x=p](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x%3Dp) .Moreover,there is also no
general guarantee that,even if it does converge at some ![x\\neq
p](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x%5Cneq+p) ,its sum is actually equal to
![f\(x\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%28x%29) .Real functions
![f](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f) that have,at each point ![p\\in
\\mathbb{R}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=p%5Cin+%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D) ,a
Taylar expansion that converges to ![f](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f)
for all ![x](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x) near enough to
![p](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=p) are called ![\\text{real
analytic}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ctext%7Breal+analytic%7D) on
![\\mathbb{R}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D)

In holomorphic function theory,it is natural to attempt to expand functions in
power of ![z](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z).We would expect to need
![z](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z) powers only and not
![\\bar{z}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbar%7Bz%7D) power since our
original definition of holomorphic functions was designed to rule out any
![\\bar{z}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbar%7Bz%7D) 's(in polynomial
functions in particular).Compared to thereal-variable case just discussed(
![C^{\\infty}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=C%5E%7B%5Cinfty%7D) function
from ![\\mathbb{R}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D) to
![\\mathbb{R}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D) ),the
attempt to expand a holomorphic function in power if
![z](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z) works remarkably
[well.In](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=http%3A//well.in/) fact,we shall see
that if
![f:U\\rightarrow\\mathbb{C}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%3AU%5Crightarrow%5Cmathbb%7BC%7D)
is holomorphic function on an open set
![U](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=U) and if ![P\\in
U](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P%5Cin+U) ,then the formal
![z](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z) expansion at
![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) ,

![\\sum_{n=0}^{\\infty}\\frac{f^{\(n\)}}{n!}\(z-P\)^n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Csum_%7Bn%3D0%7D%5E%7B%5Cinfty%7D%5Cfrac%7Bf%5E%7B%28n%29%7D%7D%7Bn%21%7D%28z-P%29%5En)
,

converges for all ![z](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z) in some
neighborhood of ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) and it converges to
![f\(z\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%28z%29) for all
![z](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z) near
![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) .Specifically,if
![r>0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=r%3E0) is such that
![D\(P,r\)\\subset
U](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=D%28P%2Cr%29%5Csubset+U) ,then the
series converges to ![f\(z\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%28z%29) for
all ![z](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z) in
![D\(P,r\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=D%28P%2Cr%29) .This expansion
shows a sence in which holomorphic functions are a natural generalization of
polynomials in ![z](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z) .

A
![\\mathbf{sequence}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmathbf%7Bsequence%7D)
of complex numbers is a function from
![\\{1,2,....\\}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5C%7B1%2C2%2C....%5C%7D)
to ![\\mathbb{C}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmathbb%7BC%7D) is said
to
![\\mathbf{converge}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmathbf%7Bconverge%7D)
to a limit
![\\scr{L}\\in\\mathbb{C}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cscr%7BL%7D%5Cin%5Cmathbb%7BC%7D)
if for any ![\\epsilon>0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cepsilon%3E0)
there is an ![N_0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=N_0) such that ![k\\ge
N_0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=k%5Cge+N_0) implies ![\\left|
a_k-\\scr{L}
\\right|<\\epsilon](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cleft%7C+a_k-%5Cscr%7BL%7D+%5Cright%7C%3C%5Cepsilon)
.It is frequently useful to test convergence by means of the ![\\text{Cauchy
criterion}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ctext%7BCauchy+criterion%7D)
:

![\\mathbf{Lemma}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmathbf%7BLemma%7D)
Let
![\\{a_k\\}_{k=1}^{\\infty}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5C%7Ba_k%5C%7D_%7Bk%3D1%7D%5E%7B%5Cinfty%7D)
be a sequence of complex numbers.Then
![\\{a_k\\}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5C%7Ba_k%5C%7D) converges to
a limit if and only if for each
![\\epsilon>0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cepsilon%3E0) there is an
![N_0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=N_0) such that ![j,k\\ge
N_0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=j%2Ck%5Cge+N_0) implies ![\\left| a_j-
a_k \\right|<\\epsilon](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cleft%7C+a_j-
a_k+%5Cright%7C%3C%5Cepsilon) .

Now lets define complex power series formally.

![\\mathbf{Definition}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmathbf%7BDefinition%7D)

Let
![P\\in\\mathbb{C}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P%5Cin%5Cmathbb%7BC%7D)
be fixed.A ![\\text{complex power
series}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ctext%7Bcomplex+power+series%7D)
(centered at ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) )is an expression of
the form

![\\sum_{k=0}^{\\infty}a_k\(z-P\)^k](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5E%7B%5Cinfty%7Da_k%28z-P%29%5Ek)

![Proof.](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=Proof.)

We first consider the complex exponential,which we
![define](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=define) as follows:

![\(1\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%281%29) If
![z=x](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z%3Dx) is real,then

![e^z=e^x\\equiv\\sum_{j=0}^{\\infty}\\frac{x^j}{j!}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=e%5Ez%3De%5Ex%5Cequiv%5Csum_%7Bj%3D0%7D%5E%7B%5Cinfty%7D%5Cfrac%7Bx%5Ej%7D%7Bj%21%7D)
as in calculus.

![\(2\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%282%29) If ![z=iy
](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z%3Diy+) is pure imaginary.then

![e^z=e^{x+iy}\\equiv
cos\(y\)+isin\(y\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=e%5Ez%3De%5E%7Bx%2Biy%7D%5Cequiv+cos%28y%29%2Bisin%28y%29)
.

![\(3\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%283%29) If
![z=x+iy](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z%3Dx%2Biy) ,then

![e=e^{x+iy}\\equiv e^x\\cdot
\(cos\(y\)+isin\(y\)\)\\](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=e%3De%5E%7Bx%2Biy%7D%5Cequiv+e%5Ex%5Ccdot+%28cos%28y%29%2Bisin%28y%29%29%5C)

Parts ![\(2\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%282%29) and
![\(3\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%283%29) of the definition,due to
![\\text{Euler}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ctext%7BEuler%7D) ,may
seem somewhat arbitrary.We shall now show,using power series,that these
definition are perferctly natural.Power series arguments that we introduced
above should be considered purely formal and given primarily for motivation.

Since,as was noted in ![\(1\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%281%29) ,we
have

![e^x=\\sum_{j=0}^{\\infty}\\frac{x^j}{j!}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=e%5Ex%3D%5Csum_%7Bj%3D0%7D%5E%7B%5Cinfty%7D%5Cfrac%7Bx%5Ej%7D%7Bj%21%7D)

then it is natural to attempt to define

![e^z=\\sum_{j=0}^{\\infty}\\frac{z^j}{j!}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=e%5Ez%3D%5Csum_%7Bj%3D0%7D%5E%7B%5Cinfty%7D%5Cfrac%7Bz%5Ej%7D%7Bj%21%7D)
![\(*\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28%2A%29)

If we assume that this series converges in some reasonable sence and that it
can be manipulated like the real power series with which we are familiar,then
we can proceed as follows:

If ![z=iy](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z%3Diy) ,
![y\\in\\mathbb{R}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=y%5Cin%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D)
,then

![e^{iy}=\\sum_{j=0}^{\\infty}\\frac{\(iy\)^j}{j!}=1+iy-\\frac{y^2}{2!}-\\frac{iy^3}{3!}+\\frac{y^4}{4!}+\\frac{iy^5}{5!}-\\frac{y^6}{6!}-\\frac{iy^7}{7!}+.....](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=e%5E%7Biy%7D%3D%5Csum_%7Bj%3D0%7D%5E%7B%5Cinfty%7D%5Cfrac%7B%28iy%29%5Ej%7D%7Bj%21%7D%3D1%2Biy-%5Cfrac%7By%5E2%7D%7B2%21%7D-%5Cfrac%7Biy%5E3%7D%7B3%21%7D%2B%5Cfrac%7By%5E4%7D%7B4%21%7D%2B%5Cfrac%7Biy%5E5%7D%7B5%21%7D-%5Cfrac%7By%5E6%7D%7B6%21%7D-%5Cfrac%7Biy%5E7%7D%7B7%21%7D%2B.....)
![=\(1-\\frac{y^2}{2!}+\\frac{y^4}{4!}-\\frac{y^6}{6!}+\\frac{y^8}{8!}-...\)+i\(y-\\frac{y^3}{3!}+\\frac{y^5}{5!}-\\frac{y^7}{7!}-...\)=cos\(y\)+isin\(y\).](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%3D%281-%5Cfrac%7By%5E2%7D%7B2%21%7D%2B%5Cfrac%7By%5E4%7D%7B4%21%7D-%5Cfrac%7By%5E6%7D%7B6%21%7D%2B%5Cfrac%7By%5E8%7D%7B8%21%7D-...%29%2Bi%28y-%5Cfrac%7By%5E3%7D%7B3%21%7D%2B%5Cfrac%7By%5E5%7D%7B5%21%7D-%5Cfrac%7By%5E7%7D%7B7%21%7D-...%29%3Dcos%28y%29%2Bisin%28y%29.)

This is the great Euler formula，when we plug in
![y=\\pi](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=y%3D%5Cpi) into the formula,we
can so that get the ![“\\text{The most beautiful
equation}"](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%E2%80%9C%5Ctext%7BThe+most+beautiful+equation%7D%22)
![e^{i\\pi}=-1\\Rightarrow
e^{i\\pi}+1=0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=e%5E%7Bi%5Cpi%7D%3D-1%5CRightarrow+e%5E%7Bi%5Cpi%7D%2B1%3D0)
,As require.

By formal manipulation of series,it is now easily checked,using the definition
![\(*\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28%2A%29) .that

![e^{a+b}=e^ae^b](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=e%5E%7Ba%2Bb%7D%3De%5Eae%5Eb)
,for any
![a,b\\in\\mathbb{C}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a%2Cb%5Cin%5Cmathbb%7BC%7D)
.

Then for ![z=x+iy](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z%3Dx%2Biy) we have

![e^z=e^{x+iy}=e^xe^{iy}=e^x\(cos\(y\)+isin\(y\)\)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=e%5Ez%3De%5E%7Bx%2Biy%7D%3De%5Exe%5E%7Biy%7D%3De%5Ex%28cos%28y%29%2Bisin%28y%29%29)
,

giving thus a formal"demonstration"of our definition of exponential.

A consequence of our definition of the complex exponential is that if
![\\zeta\\in\\mathbb{C},\\left| \\zeta
\\right|=1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Czeta%5Cin%5Cmathbb%7BC%7D%2C%5Cleft%7C+%5Czeta+%5Cright%7C%3D1)
,then there is a number
![\\theta,0\\le\\theta<2\\pi,](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ctheta%2C0%5Cle%5Ctheta%3C2%5Cpi%2C)
such that
![\\zeta=e^{i\\theta}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Czeta%3De%5E%7Bi%5Ctheta%7D)
.Here ![\\theta](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ctheta) is the
(signed)angle between the positive ![x](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x)
axis and the ray
![\\overrightarrow{0\\zeta}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Coverrightarrow%7B0%5Czeta%7D)
.

Now if ![z](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z) is any nonzero complex
number,then

![z=\\left| z\\right|\\cdot\(\\frac{z}{\\left| z\\right|}\)=\\left|
z\\right|\\cdot\\zeta](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z%3D%5Cleft%7C+z%5Cright%7C%5Ccdot%28%5Cfrac%7Bz%7D%7B%5Cleft%7C+z%5Cright%7C%7D%29%3D%5Cleft%7C+z%5Cright%7C%5Ccdot%5Czeta)

where ![\\zeta=\\frac{z}{\\left|
z\\right|}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Czeta%3D%5Cfrac%7Bz%7D%7B%5Cleft%7C+z%5Cright%7C%7D)
has modulus 1.Again letting
![\\theta](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ctheta) be the angle between
the real axis and
![\\overrightarrow{0\\zeta}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Coverrightarrow%7B0%5Czeta%7D)
,

![z=\\left| z
\\right|e^{i\\theta}=re^{i\\theta}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z%3D%5Cleft%7C+z+%5Cright%7Ce%5E%7Bi%5Ctheta%7D%3Dre%5E%7Bi%5Ctheta%7D)
,where ![r=\\left| z
\\right|](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=r%3D%5Cleft%7C+z+%5Cright%7C)
.This form is calles the
![\\text{polar}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ctext%7Bpolar%7D)
representation of the complex number
![z](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=z) .

If ![k](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=k) is an integer,then

![e^{i\\theta}=cos\(\\theta\)+isin\(\\theta\)=cos\(\\theta+2k\\pi\)+isin\(\\theta+2k\\pi\)=e^{i\(\\theta+2k\\pi\)}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=e%5E%7Bi%5Ctheta%7D%3Dcos%28%5Ctheta%29%2Bisin%28%5Ctheta%29%3Dcos%28%5Ctheta%2B2k%5Cpi%29%2Bisin%28%5Ctheta%2B2k%5Cpi%29%3De%5E%7Bi%28%5Ctheta%2B2k%5Cpi%29%7D)
.

So a nonzero complex number has infinitely many different representations of
the form
![re^{i\\theta},r>0,\\theta\\in\\mathbb{R}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=re%5E%7Bi%5Ctheta%7D%2Cr%3E0%2C%5Ctheta%5Cin%5Cmathbb%7BR%7D)
.
